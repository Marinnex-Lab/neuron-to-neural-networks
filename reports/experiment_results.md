## Interpretation

- AND and OR are linearly separable: the perceptron reaches 0 mistakes and stays there.
- XOR is not linearly separable (see docs/learning_roadmap.md, Module 3). The mistake count never reaches 0, so accuracy stays below 100%.
- Observations are limited to these runs and this learning rate. They are not a general claim about perceptron training.
