## Interpretation

- The comparison uses held-out validation data, not training data.
- At learning rate 0.05 the mean training loss after 1500 steps is 0.6235, so that setting has not converged within this step budget. Part of the difference between learning rates is therefore speed, not final quality.
- Five seeds give a rough spread, not a confidence interval.
- The result applies to this dataset, this architecture and this step count only.
