## Interpretation

- A single perceptron cannot solve XOR (see the logic-gates experiment).
- With a hidden layer, the same XOR data is solved by gradient descent for most seeds.
- Success depends on the random start: some runs with 2 hidden units get stuck, so the number of successes is reported, not only the best run.
- These results are for one dataset, one learning rate and one step count. They do not establish how often this works for other problems.
